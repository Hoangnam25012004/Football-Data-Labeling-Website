    Process

    From one video file
    to numbers you can use

    Three steps, and you only take part in the first one. The rest is our analysts and the tagging system we built to feed them.

    01 — SEND FOOTAGE

    You send the footage

    Footage from your Veo, or a match already up on YouTube — those are the two we work from most. Anything else is fine as long as the whole pitch stays in frame. Send the file, or just send the link.

    02 — WE TAG IT

    We tag every event

    Each event is recorded with the player who played it, the player who received it, pitch coordinates and a timestamp to the second. Shots also carry the point the ball crossed the line.

    03 — YOU GET IT BACK

    It lands in your channel

    Within 24 hours: the match report, a profile for every player, shot maps and passing networks — plus the raw XLSX and CSV for whoever does your analysis.

    Product

    Six things the staff open
    on Monday morning

    Match report

    Both sides across 60+ metrics, a timeline of goals, cards and substitutions, and the starting shape each team lined up in.

    Shot map

    Every shot carries where it was struck from and where it crossed the line. The goal stands beside the pitch, so it reads as one thing: struck here, ended up there.

    Passing network

    Who passed to whom and how often, in fifteen-minute windows. The full matrix, plus the network drawn on the pitch it happened on.

    Player profile

    Every metric we count for that player, a touch heat map for each half, and a profile radar that puts the shape of their game on one picture.

    Defensive map

    Tackles, interceptions, duels, fouls and offsides plotted where they actually happened, split by half so you can see the line push up or drop off.

    Exports

    Every player's match as one workbook: Shooting, Distribution, Defensive and Other — the four tables you read on screen — for both squads, eight sheets in all. XLSX for Excel, one CSV with the eight stacked inside it, PDF for the report itself.
